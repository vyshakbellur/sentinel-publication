# Anonymous artifact appendix skeleton

## Scope

The artifact contains the frozen fault catalog, study partition, baseline and
Guardian adapters, terminal-record contracts, deterministic simulator preflight,
live Kubernetes controller contracts, analysis code, verified bibliography, and
same-host reproduction reports.

## Evidence classes

- Simulator runs validate plumbing and expose selected counterexamples; they do
  not estimate live risk.
- Recorded transports validate controller behavior; they are not Kubernetes
  executions.
- Kubernetes audit fixtures validate parsing and reconciliation contracts; they
  are not live audit calibration.
- Fresh virtual environments validate dependency reproducibility on one host;
  they are not independent replication.

## Missing release material

- bounded raw pilot, census, confirmatory, and live-ablation records;
- frozen pilot-derived repetition count and statistical model;
- regenerated tables and figures with uncertainty;
- independent-machine artifact report;
- venue-template manuscript and policy-compliant anonymous archive.

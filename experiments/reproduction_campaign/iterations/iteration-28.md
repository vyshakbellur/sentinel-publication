# Iteration 28 milestone: final claim and publication-readiness audit

Timestamp: 2026-09-29T03:38:38Z

## Research question

After 28 bounded iterations, which SENTINEL claims survive reproduction,
prior-art subtraction, adaptive development testing, boundary-fault analysis,
and the confirmatory-validity audit—and is the paper ready for arXiv, ARR, or
archival peer review?

## Competing explanations

- H1: the accumulated synthetic trial count is sufficient to support a broad
  defense-effectiveness paper.
- H2: SENTINEL has established nominal superiority over correctly labeled IFC.
- H3: no positive contribution survives, so only an artifact-replication note
  remains.
- H4: a narrow evaluation contribution survives—cross-layer effect
  correspondence and assurance cut sets—but the evidence is development-only
  and not yet publication-grade.
- H5: the manuscript can be submitted despite missing confirmation,
  uncertainty, real multi-service effects, and result placeholders.

## Smallest discriminating action

Create a write-once, hash-bound audit over milestone 16, milestone 20,
milestone 24, and iterations 25–27. Recompute compatible evidence-inventory
counts, retain an explicit non-pooling warning, classify every baseline by
reproduction grade, apply nine fail-closed publication gates, and freeze the
claim and venue decisions in both machine-readable and human-readable records.
Add no further efficacy experiment.

## Acceptance and falsification criteria

- Verify all six source-report hashes and reproduce the final audit
  byte-for-byte.
- Preserve zero published effectiveness numbers, zero exact Invitation
  scenarios, and zero valid confirmatory holdouts.
- Never convert 1,329 heterogeneous deterministic trials into a pooled rate or
  confidence interval.
- Reject novelty for deterministic boundaries, IFC/provenance enforcement,
  temporal policy, Hoare contracts, and typed action contracts.
- Reject nominal superiority over correctly labeled IFC and reject prevention
  credit for post-effect detection.
- Retain only development-supported cross-layer effect correspondence and
  minimal assurance cut sets as candidate contributions.
- Publication readiness requires every gate; any false gate yields a negative
  readiness verdict.

## Results

The final audit passed and reproduced byte-for-byte. Six source reports were
hash-verified, transitively covering the accepted campaign evidence.

### What was actually reproduced or executed

- AgentDojo public source executability, native tasks, and scorer behavior were
  validated; no paper effectiveness number was reproduced.
- CaMeL's released tests and deterministic policy paths executed; no published
  defense result was reproduced.
- Agent-C's archive and checker were replicated with documented dependency
  substitutions; no model-backed number was reproduced.
- The model-free Fides tutorial core executed. Its AgentDojo comparison remains
  a conceptual reconstruction.
- An official ToolGate artifact path executed under a deterministic probe; no
  ToolBench or MCP-Universe result was reproduced.

### What remains reconstruction or synthetic evidence

- Invitation coverage is conceptual behavioral reconstruction: 5/5 threat
  classes but 0/14 exact production scenarios.
- Fides AgentDojo results are conceptual, not an official integration.
- Calendar promptware, URL/file transitions, adaptive mutations, boundary
  faults, and the iteration-27 challenge set use deterministic inert fixtures.
- The iteration-27 cases were authored after development and are not valid
  held-out confirmation.

### Surviving and rejected claims

The primary surviving contribution is **development-supported cross-layer
effect correspondence**: the common trace and independent effect namespace
exposed scorer errors, false success, post-effect rejection, duplicate effects,
and observer omission that nominal verdicts did not capture.

The structural companion is **development-supported minimal assurance cut-set
analysis**. In the constructed SENTINEL chain, adapter and broker singletons
each defeat prevention. Silent compromise requires either prevention fault
plus observer omission. This is a structural result, not a reliability rate or
Byzantine guarantee.

Trusted complete-tuple construction remains a useful reference mechanism, not
standalone novelty. Non-transitive URL/file transitions remain an engineered
experimental factor, not a novel security model.

Five broad novelty claims remain eliminated by prior art. Nominal superiority
over correct-label IFC is rejected. General prevention from effect observation
is rejected by the duplicate-effect case. Published defense effectiveness is
unsupported.

### Publication readiness

All nine final gates are false: matched official baselines, valid held-out
confirmation, probabilistic repeats or independent blocks, uncertainty
intervals, realistic multi-service effects, independently authenticated
observation, model-backed adaptive attacks, a result-grounded manuscript, and
a completed venue/release audit.

The paper is therefore **not ready for arXiv as a research-results preprint,
ARR, a Q1 journal, or a flagship/archival conference**.

## Limitations

The campaign provides no population effect size, uncertainty, live
multi-service behavior, independently authenticated observer, official matched
Fides baseline, exact Invitation reproduction, or model-backed adaptive attack
rate. Same-host deterministic replay cannot establish external validity.

The cut-set model treats components and faults as explicit logical variables;
it does not model correlated failures, common-mode compromise, production
timing, or fault probabilities. Observers detect rather than undo completed
effects. The challenge-set failure shows that broker idempotency and
compensation are required research targets, not established SENTINEL features.

The manuscript still contains abstract, result-table, and conclusion
placeholders and is not in a venue template. Publishing it unchanged would
overstate the evidence.

## Interpretation

H1, H2, H3, and H5 are rejected; H4 is supported. The project has a credible
research direction, but not a completed empirical paper. Its strongest story
is not “a deterministic fort stops prompt injection.” It is:

> Nominally correct deterministic agent boundaries can still fail across
> trusted attribute construction, dispatch, receipts, effects, and observation;
> SENTINEL provides an effect-grounded method to expose and minimize those
> assurance failures.

The negative-results core is scientifically valuable: ideal IFC and SENTINEL
tied on nominal synthetic matrices; identifiers did not create a valid holdout;
an observer exposed but did not prevent a duplicate effect; and deterministic
trial saturation did not create statistical evidence.

## Publication impact

The best current route is an honest workshop short paper, artifact paper, or
technical report after rewriting the manuscript around bounded deterministic
results and removing all placeholders. It must be marketed as methodology plus
negative results, not a defense-effectiveness paper.

For archival publication, the natural venue class is dependable-systems or
security evaluation after matched live experiments on at least two resettable
public multi-service systems. ARR is a poor fit unless the work adds repeated,
model-backed NLP benchmark experiments. A Q1 journal or flagship conference is
premature without confirmation, uncertainty, external validity, authenticated
observation, and independent reproduction.

The documentation skill shaped the reader-first verdict, evidence-grade
separation, claim wording, blocker list, and venue-class recommendation.

## Decision

**Stop at the hard limit of 28 iterations and pause the automation.** The next
scientifically useful work requires new external evidence: live resettable
systems, official matched baselines, independently authenticated observation,
a valid executable holdout, repeated probabilistic and infrastructure blocks,
idempotency/compensation ablations, and independent reproduction. Do not spend
additional local cycles polishing unsupported empirical claims.
